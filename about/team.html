Chairman

Henry W. Hart

Henry Hart is the Chairman of The CCA, leading the organization's mission to support faithful Christians running for public office. He discovered a deep passion for Christian politics after working on Pastor Tom Gray’s congressional campaign. Outside of his leadership work, Henry is an avid runner, guitar player, and the creator of the strategy card game Consec-ya!.
