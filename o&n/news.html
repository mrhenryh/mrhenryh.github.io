Chairman & Board News

Official announcements, governance actions, and public resolutions from the leadership of the Christian Conservative Association.

November 10, 2026 Board Vote: 7–0 Unanimous

CCA Board Adopts Resolution Supporting U.S. House Reapportionment and Structural Reform

In an official executive session, the Board of Directors unanimously voted to endorse legislative frameworks aimed at expanding the size of the U.S. House of Representatives and encouraging proportional civic representation. Chairman Henry W. Hart emphasized that closer local representation is essential to restoring accountability and strengthening the American nuclear family.

October 24, 2026 Executive Action

Chairman Hart Announces 2030 National Outreach Initiative Across County Networks

Chairman Henry W. Hart has officially launched the CCA's national outreach initiative, setting an organizational target to secure grassroots representation and active subscriber networks in every county across the United States before 2030. Regional state board representatives are instructed to begin coordination immediately.

September 15, 2026 Board Vote: 6–1 Approved

Establishment of the Christian Candidate Incubator ("The Club") Portal

The Board authorized the development and deployment of the private Member Caucus and candidate support portal. Designed to offer peer-to-peer networking, model family preservation legislation, and collaborative strategy tools, the portal went live for verified officeholders and candidates.
