Understanding Membership: The Four W's

Membership in the Christian Conservative Association is an elevated responsibility reserved for committed believers serving in public life.

1. Who?

Reserved for verified Christian elected officials, candidates, pastors, and CCA staff members who have previously joined as subscribers.

2. What?

Members gain powerful privileges: a secure personal portal page, private discussion caucuses, op-ed publishing rights, and custom Passion Projects.

3. Why?

To unite faithful leaders in a trusted, peer-to-peer network dedicated to strengthening the nuclear family and bringing biblical integrity into governance.

4. How?

Submit your confidential application below directly to our Dean. Membership does not require annual renewal, though status is maintained under Board oversight.

Confidential Application Process

Privacy Assurance: Unlike public forms or third-party databases, your application is sent directly via email to our Dean without intermediaries, ensuring complete confidentiality and a personal channel for dialogue.
