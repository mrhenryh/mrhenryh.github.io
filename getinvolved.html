Why Join the Christian Conservative Association?

In an era of endless political noise, voters face a daunting challenge: discerning which candidates truly stand for godly principles and Christian stewardship. Most citizens simply do not have the time to research every local candidate on their ballot.

When you get involved with the CCA as a subscriber, you gain access to a trusted network that does the heavy lifting for you:

  • Principled Candidate Research: Rely on thorough, faith-first evaluations of candidates running in your local city, county, and state races.
  • Tailored Precinct Voter Guides: Receive localized election guides designed specifically for your precinct before you step into the voting booth.
  • Direct Community Connection: Stay connected with faithful Christian leaders, pastors, and fellow voters dedicated to restoring the nuclear family as society's foundation.
