Our Core Pillars

The Christian Conservative Association brings together grassroots voters and public leaders around three foundational commitments:

1. Strengthening the Nuclear Family

We believe the nuclear family is society's foundational institution. Healthy communities, strong churches, and flourishing governance begin with supporting marriage, parents, and children.

2. Encouraging Christian Candidates

We equip faithful Christian candidates and elected officeholders with peer support, strategy resources, model policies, and platform visibility to serve with integrity.

3. Empowering Grassroots Voters

We provide voters with clear election resources, candidate guides, and educational tools to help citizens make informed decisions at the ballot box.
