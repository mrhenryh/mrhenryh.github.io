Stepping Forward to Serve: Run for Office

Faith first, politics second. We encourage Christian men and women to step into the public square, covered in prayer, to represent godly principles and serve as lights in their local communities.

Who Should Inquire Here?

Please Note: This interest inquiry form is designed exclusively for prospective office-seekers and Christian citizens considering a run for local, state, or federal office.

If you fit one of the categories below, please use our direct contact channels instead:

  • Already Endorsed Candidates & Verified Members: Please do not submit this form. Contact our Dean directly or email our Secretary.
  • Candidates Seeking Official Endorsement: Candidates seeking an official endorsement from the Chairman should utilize the Endorsement Contact box below.

Why Christian Leadership Matters

Scripture calls believers to be the salt of the earth and the light of the world. Running for office is not about seeking personal status—it is a ministry of public stewardship.

1. Protecting the Family

The nuclear family is society's primary building block. Christian leaders are needed to defend parental authority, marriage, and the well-being of children at every level of local governance.

2. Covered in Prayer & Support

You do not have to stand alone. Our association provides spiritual encouragement, pastoral guidance, and a supportive network committed to praying for your walk and your family.

3. Being a Light Everywhere

We do not measure faithful service solely by political victory. Even in heavily leaning opposition districts, running as a faithful Christian brings truth, integrity, and gospel witness to public debate.

Discerning Your Calling & Vocation

When considering public office, we encourage candidates to examine their professional gifts, daily experience, and vocational background to match the right level of public service:

  • School Boards: Ideal for teachers, educators, parents, and Christian school administrators who understand classroom dynamics and curriculum stewardship.
  • City Council & County Commission: Ideal for local small business owners, tradesmen, landowners, and ministry leaders who manage budgets and care about community safety.
  • State House & Senate: Ideal for attorneys, policy advocates, civic leaders, and experienced local officials prepared to craft state legislation.
  • Running to Be a Light: We actively encourage candidates in all districts—regardless of political breakdown—to step forward so every voter has a clear Christian conservative choice on their ballot.
