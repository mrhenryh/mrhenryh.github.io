Community Guidelines & Terms of Use

Welcome to our public discussion community. By submitting a post or reply here, you agree to the following netiquette rules and moderation terms:

  • Civil Discourse & Netiquette: Please keep all contributions respectful, constructive, and relevant to the discussion topic. Personal attacks, profanity, hate speech, and off-topic arguments are not permitted.
  • No Impersonation: You may not use a display name that falsely represents a public figure, elected official, political candidate, or another individual. Unverified posts claiming official representation will be denied or removed.
  • Moderator & Editor-in-Chief Authority: The CCA Editor-in-Chief and site moderators reserve the absolute right, at their sole discretion, to deny, edit, hide, or remove any comment, topic, or display name at any time without prior notice if it is deemed inappropriate, off-topic, spam, or in violation of community standards.
  • User-Generated Content Disclaimer: Comments and viewpoints expressed on this forum reflect the individual opinions of the posters and do not represent an official position of the Christian Conservative Association.
  • Appeals & Inquiries: If you believe a comment was denied or removed in error, or if you wish to report impersonation or misconduct, please reach out to our moderation team via our Contact Page.
How Posting Works: All submissions are reviewed by moderators for civility and identity verification before publication. Thank you for contributing to thoughtful dialogue.
