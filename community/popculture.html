Culture & Connections

A gathering place for musicians, pastors, public servants, moms, dads, scientists, and teens alike—proving that when believers show up in real life, community comes first and good policy follows naturally.

People First. Policy as a Byproduct.

Most political organizations are built around rallies, talking points, and winning the next election cycle. The CCA was built to be an online home for the church—a place where joy, authenticity, and real relationships lead the way. Our ultimate goal isn't the presidency; it's strengthening the nuclear family and uniting believers across every walk of life.

Whether it's a Christian teen in Iowa, a worship artist on tour, a local pastor, a disaster-relief volunteer, or a governor serving their state, there is nothing quite like believers walking together in public life. They are approachable, relational, and deeply invested in their neighbors.
